Information from quote and contact forms

If you submit the quote form, we receive the details you enter, such as name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, service type, timing, and job notes. If you email or call us, we also receive whatever you choose to send.

We use that information to respond to your request, provide a quote, and schedule service. We do not sell it. We do not add it to unrelated marketing lists.

Online form submissions are processed by Web3Forms when a production access key is installed. Web3Forms then delivers the message to our email inbox. If the form cannot send, you may be offered an option to open your own email app instead.

Phone calls

Phone numbers on this site use click-to-call links (tel:4158608154). If you tap or click those links, your device places or prepares a call in the usual way. We do not collect extra data from the link itself beyond a possible analytics event (see below) that a call link was used.

Analytics

This site is prepared for Google Analytics 4 and/or Google Tag Manager. Those tools are not active until the business inserts real measurement IDs in tracking.js.

When enabled, they typically use cookies or similar storage to understand page views and events such as a successful quote submission. Google’s own privacy documentation applies to data they process.

Advertising and conversion tracking

The site is also prepared for Google Ads conversion tags for successful quote submissions and click-to-call. Those tags stay off until Ads IDs and conversion labels are added in tracking.js.

If enabled, Google Ads may use cookies or similar technologies to measure whether a site visit later led to a form submit or a tap on the phone number.

Cookies and third-party services

Depending on configuration, this website may load:

  • Google Fonts, to display site typography (a request to Google’s font servers).
  • Web3Forms, to deliver quote-form messages.
  • Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4, and/or Google Ads, only after IDs are installed.

Social profile links (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) go to those sites when you choose to open them. We do not embed those networks’ widgets on this privacy page.
